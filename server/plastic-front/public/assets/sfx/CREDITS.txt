Edits: individual-shot extraction, mono conversion, trim/fades, filtering,
pitch changes, peak normalization and 44.1 kHz / 96 kbit/s MP3 encoding.
These are designed game sound effects, not authentic recordings of every
named weapon. No music is included. Original assets remain with their authors.

v0.32: sniper preserves the recorded rifle attack with 26 ms lead-in trim,
gentle filtering, a short low-level echo and peak normalization. Rocket is
rebuilt from the same existing cannon-derived tank sample with a slightly
higher pitch, fuller low mids and a short tail. No tank, rifle, machinegun,
truck, mortar, impact or artillery files were changed. Recipe:
tools/refresh-heavy-sfx.py (ffmpeg, numpy). Both revised sounds are mono
44.1 kHz / 128 kbit/s MP3; runtime loudness stays behind the existing master
volume, compressor, distance falloff, pause/mute and voice-count limits.

adjacent recorded attacks at 0.066–0.234 s of the supplied MP3. Pitch-preserving
atempo=0.84 spaces attacks about 100 ms apart; 0.2 s output with a 15 ms tail

reports/s, twice v0.33's 5 reports/s, without doubling game damage. This keeps
the recorded inter-shot body instead of padding a chopped single shot. Other
