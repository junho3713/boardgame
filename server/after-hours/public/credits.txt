Generation prompt:
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a high-detail exterior matte painting texture for the window of an existing real-time 3D mystery escape-room game, AFTER HOURS, room 316. Generate a very wide landscape image, approximately 2:1, ideally 3072 by 1536. Primary request: a believable quiet rainy night view over an older Korean coastal town, seen from the third floor of a modest hotel. ONLY the outdoor environment: no window frame, no glass surface, no interior, no curtains, no border. Eye level roughly 8 metres above street level, looking nearly horizontally with a slight view down onto nearby rooftops. Composition: panoramic urban environment with several detailed 3–5-storey old brick and weathered plaster buildings across a narrow damp street/courtyard, some nearer facades on the sides and a clear central view past tiled and flat rooftops into layers of lower buildings, distant dark coastal hills and faint mist. Buildings should have believable imperfect proportions and architectural detail: recessed dark window panes and a few dim amber occupied windows, concrete cornices, narrow balconies, roof parapets, drainpipes, rain-darkened brickwork, modest rooftop equipment and antennas. Fine natural material detail, physically believable reflections on wet roof surfaces and faint street below. A few subtle power lines belong to the distant setting. Upper quarter is a low overcast blue-grey night sky, no dramatic moon. Lighting/mood: dark but legible rainy late evening, muted blue-grey ambient light with restrained warm window light, melancholic and ordinary, suitable behind the window of a warm old hotel room. Style: photorealistic environmental photography, natural weathered surfaces, realistic spatial depth, restrained contrast, no painterly brushwork, no toy buildings, no simplified cubes, no cinematic fantasy, no neon spectacle. Avoid people, cars in the foreground, readable signs, text, logos, stars, watermarks. Do not paint large rain streaks or raindrops over the image; rain will be animated separately in 3D. Keep the entire frame filled with scenery that can be sampled on a curved distant backdrop.

Spherical environment revision (2026-11-17): built-in OpenAI imagegen edit using the original rainy-town image as a visual reference. The full environment replaces the bounded photographic band.
Revision prompt:
Use case: lighting-weather / environment projection repair. Input image: visual reference for the existing fictional rainy Korean coastal town, its architecture, lighting and colour. Produce a NEW full 360-degree by 180-degree EQUIRECTANGULAR ENVIRONMENT MAP, exactly 2:1 landscape, suitable for mapping onto the inside of a sphere in a Three.js game. This is not an ordinary cropped city photograph and not a circular fisheye image. The horizon must be a straight continuous line at exactly 50 percent image height, vertical architectural lines should have the expected equirectangular perspective, left and right edges must join seamlessly. Viewpoint is suspended 8 metres above a narrow wet lane in an older Korean coastal neighbourhood, outside a third-floor hotel window, looking across an open small courtyard. Keep the same photorealistic weathered brick and peeling plaster buildings, subdued rainy night atmosphere, a few dim amber occupied windows, rooftop antennas and the distant dark sea and hills from the reference. At the horizontal centre, leave an unobstructed coherent view across the courtyard toward detailed 3–4-storey facades, lower roofs, and distant town; avoid a huge close wall blocking the centre. Upper half: continuous clouded blue-grey night sky with the upper parts of roof silhouettes at the horizon, no dramatic stars or moon. Lower half: COMPLETE believable descending view, with rain-darkened roofs, full walls and a wet paved courtyard/lane continuing all the way down to the nadir. The very bottom must be real textured damp paving in the projection, not a black band, empty void, stretched pixels, crop edge, blur or rectangular colour patch. Continuous detailed physical environment in every direction; no pasted low-poly blocks, no disconnected floating buildings, no straight image cutoff. Restrained lighting, realistic urban material detail, cinematic realism without spectacle. Do not include any hotel interior, window frame, glass, curtains, railing immediately in front of the viewer, camera, tripod, people, visible readable signage, logo or watermark. Do not add large rain streaks across the picture; rain is rendered separately. No illustration, no toy buildings, no poster border. Create the equirectangular texture itself, filling the entire rectangular frame.
